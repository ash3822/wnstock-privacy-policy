Privacy Policy for Wnstock

Last Updated: 14 November 2026

Wnstock ("we", "our", or "us") values your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use the Wnstock mobile application.


Information We Collect

Account Information

When you create an account or sign in, we may collect:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Profile photo (when using Google Sign-In)

Authentication is provided through Firebase Authentication.


User Content

Information that you voluntarily upload or submit to the app, including:

  • Profile information
  • Images uploaded to the application
  • Messages and other content you choose to share

Images are stored using Cloudinary.


Call Data

The application supports voice and video calling through LiveKit and WebRTC technologies.

During calls, audio and video data are transmitted to enable real-time communication. We do not record audio or video calls unless explicitly stated within the application.


Device and Usage Information

We may automatically collect:

  • Device type
  • Operating system version
  • Application usage data
  • Crash reports
  • Diagnostic information

Advertising Information

The application displays advertisements through Google AdMob.

AdMob may collect information such as:

  • Advertising ID
  • Device information
  • Approximate location
  • Usage information

This information may be used to provide and improve advertising services, including personalised advertisements where permitted.


How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

  • Create and manage user accounts
  • Authenticate users
  • Store and retrieve user content
  • Enable voice and video communication
  • Improve application performance
  • Provide customer support
  • Display advertisements
  • Maintain security and prevent abuse

Data Storage and Third-Party Services

We use trusted third-party service providers, including:

  • Firebase (Google LLC) for authentication and database services
  • Cloudinary for image storage and delivery
  • LiveKit for real-time video communication
  • WebRTC technologies for voice communication
  • Google AdMob for advertising services

These providers may process information according to their own privacy policies.


Data Sharing

We do not sell users' personal information.

Information may be shared only:

  • With service providers necessary for app functionality
  • When required by law
  • To protect the rights, safety, or security of users and the application

Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect user information. However, no method of electronic storage or internet transmission can guarantee absolute security.


Children's Privacy

The application is not directed toward children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If we learn that personal information from a child has been collected, we will take reasonable steps to remove such information.


Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to:

  • Access your personal information
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your account and personal data
  • Withdraw consent where applicable

Account Deletion

Users may request account deletion by contacting us using the information below. Upon verification, we will remove account information as permitted by law and operational requirements.
